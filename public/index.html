Selected work

LEDRING

Status lights for AI agents. It replays agent traces from Claude Code or OpenTelemetry GenAI as one branch tree, and scores how far a run drifted from what it was asked to do.

LEDRING Play is a browser game built on top of it. You watch CX-417, an AI builder, turn your idea into a web page in real time. It may cut corners. You approve, push back, or stop it, and every choice branches toward one of seven endings. The built-in AI needs no key or sign-up, and the game is in English and Chinese.
